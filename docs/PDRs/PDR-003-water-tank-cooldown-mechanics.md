# PDR-003: Water Tank with Automatic 2-Second Cooldown

**Date**: 2026-10-31
**Status**: Proposed
**Author(s) / Decision Owner**: User & TP AIDLC Assistant
**Target Personas**: Children, Casual Players
**Related Issues / Epics**: [Product Requirements](../../aidlc-docs/inception/product-requirements/product-requirements.md)
**Supersedes**: None
**Superseded by**: None

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Context

Unbounded continuous shooting can lead to spam clicking that trivializes cat hiding mechanics. However, complex manual reload buttons can frustrate young players.

---

## 2. Decision Drivers & Business Goals

- Encourage rhythmic, thoughtful aiming over constant spray.
- Keep controls frictionless without requiring extra keys or secondary reload clicks.
- Clear visual indicator for water reserve.

---

## 3. Options Evaluated

### Option 1: Water Tank with Automatic 2-Second Cooldown (Selected)
A prominent colorful water meter at the bottom of the screen. Holds enough water for ~15-20 rapid bubble shots. When depleted, a gentle bubble-refill animation triggers and refills to full in 2 seconds.

**Pros:**
- Simple, intuitive rhythm. No complicated reload buttons.

**Cons & Risks:**
- Brief downtime when empty.

### Option 2: Infinite Ammo with Zero Cooldown
Hold down mouse button indefinitely.

**Why not selected:**
- Reduces the game to holding down click, removing pacing and tactical observation.

---

## 4. Decision Outcome & Rationale

Selected Option 1. The 2-second automatic refill is gentle, kid-friendly, and introduces a satisfying gameplay loop.
